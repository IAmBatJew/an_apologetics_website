What is the Christian Perspective?

Christian Perspective

Being homosexual is not a "sin". The reality is that the sinful part is not much different than that of a heterosexual person, acting on innappropriate sexual desires. You are allowed to love another person of the same gender, in fact, we are called to love them. However, to marry, make love to, or kiss him/her is a different matter entirely. People say that people are born this way and that it is totally natural. This is not relevant. We are called to love all people and walk alongside them in their struggles. All of this to say: people are brought to repentance via love and kindness, not condemnation. It is not our place to tell a person they are sinful. We are created "very good," but we perform acts that are sinful. To clarify, it is not biblical to say that a person is sinful, but rather that they have performed sinful acts. One is a value statement. The other is an observation. Those of us who deal with homosexuality are still encouraged to have deep and meaningful relationships with people of the same gender. The limit comes in the physical intimacy space. The same goes for heterosexuals. We are only allowed physical intimacy with our spouse, and no one else.
